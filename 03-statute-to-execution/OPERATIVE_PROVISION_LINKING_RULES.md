# Operative Provision Linking Rules

A claim of statutory authorization must identify the operative provision and the claimed effect. A citation to a bill title, agency summary, press release, policy memorandum, or general subject area is insufficient.

The link must preserve scope, time applicability, conditions, exceptions, and the relationship between the provision and the proposed execution. Where an interpretation is necessary, it is a separate, attributed inputâ€”not a silent merge into source.

This rule does not determine statutory meaning. It prevents a statutory citation from becoming an unexplained shortcut to effect.
