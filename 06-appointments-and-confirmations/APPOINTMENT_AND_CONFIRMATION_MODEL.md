# Appointment and Confirmation Model

Appointment records preserve the route by which a person may occupy an office. Confirmation, election, appointment, acting designation, commission, oath, vacancy, removal, and succession are separate events. They must not collapse into the assertion that someone was simply "in charge."

For a material office, the record identifies office, holder, mechanism, any required confirmation or election, effect time, acting-status basis, and official source locator. This model records provenance; it does not decide a contested appointment.
