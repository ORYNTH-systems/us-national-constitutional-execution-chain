Federal Judicial Appointment, Tenure, and Removal Boundary

Nomination, Senate advice and consent, appointment or commission, oath, entry into office, good-behaviour tenure, compensation protection, assignment, disability, vacancy, impeachment, conviction, and removal are distinct events. No name or title proves any event.

Sources: article-02-section-02, article-03-section-01, article-i-section-02, article-i-section-03, and article-06-unit-01. Current population: UNADMITTED.
