Federal Judicial Officeholder Source Requirements

Nomination, Senate action, appointment, commission, oath, entry into service, court assignment, designation, senior status, disability, vacancy, impeachment, conviction, removal, resignation, retirement, and death are separately sourced events. A biography, directory entry, title, portrait, press release, or observed conduct cannot establish the complete officeholder chain.
