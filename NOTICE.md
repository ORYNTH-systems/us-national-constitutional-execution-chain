# Public Reference Notice

This repository is a public reference candidate for the U.S. National Constitutional Execution Chain.

Its purpose is to make the structure of an execution-integrity system inspectable: constitutional source, office, jurisdiction, enacted authority, delegation, procedure and evidence, decision, execution, receipt, review, and remedy.

It does not create legal power, decide constitutional meaning, act for a public institution, determine rights, or make a governmental effect valid.

Original ORYNTH material is available under the repository LICENSE. Public visibility permits inspection, evaluation, and citation; it does not confer a general reuse, implementation, commercialization, deployment, or derivative-work right.

Official-source snapshots are retained solely as provenance artifacts for the indexed source records. They are identified separately from original ORYNTH material and remain subject to their own source provenance and applicable terms.
