# Public Trace Minimum

For a consequential public action, the public-safe trace should identify:

- what action or effect is at issue;
- the constitutional/statutory and institutional route claimed;
- the responsible office or principal;
- applicable jurisdiction and time boundary;
- decision and procedure state;
- whether material evidence/predicates are resolved, contested, or restricted;
- the execution/effect receipt; and
- how a person may seek review, challenge, correction, or remedy.

If information is lawfully restricted, publish the restriction's attributable basis and review route to the extent permitted. Do not misdescribe absence of public detail as proof that a required chain link exists.
