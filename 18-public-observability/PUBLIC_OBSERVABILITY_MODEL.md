# Public Observability Model

Public understanding requires more than publication volume. A consequential action should expose a legible, time-aware trace: source references, actor and office, jurisdiction, authority/delegation links, procedure state, evidence status, decision, effect receipt, and review/remedy route.

Observability is bounded by lawful privacy, security, classification, due-process, and evidentiary constraints. A protected fact is not converted into public material by this repository. Instead, the system records that a restriction exists, who controls it, its basis, scope, review condition, and any public-safe description.

Opacity cannot be used to manufacture authority. A necessary restriction remains itself attributable and reviewable through its defined lawful route.
