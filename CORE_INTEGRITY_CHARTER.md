# Core Integrity Charter

## Purpose

This repository is a non-authoritative operational-integrity layer for making consequential United States public action inspectable from constitutional source to real-world effect. It does not create, transfer, enlarge, or conclusively interpret legal authority.

The Constitution remains the binding source. Lawful institutions retain their constitutional and statutory roles. This layer records the chain through which an asserted public action becomes attributable, bounded, evidenced, reviewable, and correctable.

## The integrity objective

No consequential public effect may be treated as complete merely because an institution, official, contractor, or system produced it. The action must be capable of reconstruction through its lawful source, competent actor, jurisdiction, enacted authority, delegation, procedure, evidence, decision, execution, receipt, and review/remedy path.

## Influence boundary

The objective is not to eliminate lawful democratic participation, advocacy, expert input, petition, voting, oversight, or judicial review. It is to prevent undisclosed, untraceable, stale, or unauthorized influence from silently becoming governmental effect.

An input may inform an action. It cannot substitute for constitutional source, lawful authority, required procedure, material evidence, or a reviewable decision.

## Core rules

1. Source is not interpretation.
2. An institution is not automatically the competent actor for a particular action.
3. An actor is not automatically within jurisdiction.
4. A statute is not automatically a current authorization for a particular effect.
5. Delegation can narrow authority but cannot create or enlarge it.
6. Evidence can support a decision but cannot adjudicate itself.
7. Execution is not proof of lawful effect.
8. Every consequential effect requires a durable receipt and an available lawful review/remedy route.
9. A material unknown, conflict, revocation, expiry, or unresolved predicate prevents an integrity finding of admissibility for consequence.

## Scope

The same chain applies across legislation, appropriations, appointments, agency action, emergency authority, procurement, external execution, courts, elections, automated systems, and corrective processes. Congress is the first legislative-origin module, not the whole system.
