# Source Snapshot Publication Boundary

The tracked files under `records/official-source-snapshots/` are preserved source representations used to support reproducible source identification, structural indexing, attestation, and later audit.

They are not original ORYNTH content. Their presence does not assert ownership, alter the source, state a constitutional interpretation, or grant reuse rights in source material.

Each source record and attestation remains responsible for identifying its acquisition event, source location, retrieval context, and hash. A public reader must distinguish source material, ORYNTH structural records, and later interpretation or implementation work.
