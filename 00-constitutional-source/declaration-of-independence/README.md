# Declaration of Independence — foundational source extension

Status: PROPOSED / NOT RELEASED. This extension does not alter the v1.0.0 reference baseline.

The Declaration is a historical and political foundation, not an independent grant of enforceable federal authority. Constitutional authority remains subject to the Constitution, ratification, and applicable law.

Source acquisition uses the existing `00-constitutional-source/SOURCE_INGESTION_PROTOCOL.md`. No canonical source is represented as retrieved, hashed, or verified until an acquisition record demonstrates it.

The Declaration's grievances, principles, and crosswalks are kept separate from official-source snapshots. Crosswalks are analytical hypotheses, not legal holdings. Never infer legality from historical analogy.
