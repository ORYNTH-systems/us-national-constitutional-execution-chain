# Declaration authority boundary

1. Source text and metadata do not create legal authority.
2. Foundational principle != constitutional provision != binding adjudication.
3. An interpretive crosswalk may be recorded as PROPOSED or REVIEWED, never as automatic authorization.
4. No finding of legal invalidity may derive solely from the Declaration or a historical grievance analogy.
5. A binding-law claim requires an independently cited operative legal basis and its own verification status.
6. Missing text, disputed provenance, or uncertain crosswalks remain explicitly unresolved.
7. This repository is an independent reference system, not a government decision-maker.
