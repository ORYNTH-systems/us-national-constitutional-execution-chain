Constitutional Source Scope Boundary

Private structural boundary. The unified registry includes the Preamble, Articles I-VII, and ratified Amendments I-XXVII. It preserves their separation from page metadata and attestation material.

Included

  • Preamble as one structural source unit.
  • Article sections where the signed Constitution supplies section headings.
  • Whole-article units for Articles V-VII, which contain no numbered sections in the source.
  • Amendment sections where NARA supplies section headings.
  • Whole-amendment units where no numbered sections appear.

Preserved but not registered as operative units

  • Framer signatures and attestation text.
  • Proposal and ratification dates.
  • NARA editorial, supersession, modification, capitalization, and navigation notes.
  • Unratified proposed articles appearing in the enrolled Bill of Rights transcription.

Effect boundary

Structural inclusion does not determine legal operation. Structural exclusion does not delete source material; canonical representations remain preserved and hash-bound.
