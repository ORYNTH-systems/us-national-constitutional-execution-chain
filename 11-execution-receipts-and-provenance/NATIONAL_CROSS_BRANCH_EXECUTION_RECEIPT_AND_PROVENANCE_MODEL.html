National Cross-Branch Execution Receipt and Provenance Model

Every attempted hold, decision, remedy, correction, release, reversal, reopening, or downstream effect requires a receipt preserving the input record, source authority, actor identity and capacity, jurisdiction, event time, effective time, decision state, conflicts, counterfindings, affected objects, output state, and cryptographic identity.

A missing receipt blocks consequential execution. A later receipt supplements history; it never overwrites the earlier trace.
