Non-operational workflow walkthrough

Illustrative example only. This is not an account of a real government record, person, institution, decision, or effect.

Scenario

An institution receives a document said to support a proposed administrative action.

  1. Acquire: preserve the received artifact and its acquisition receipt; do not call it authoritative.
  2. Classify: record the document as a source-bound proposal with provenance, extraction limits, and time context.
  3. Test: compare the proposal against required authority, authenticity, effective-time, and conflict predicates.
  4. Route: if prerequisites are incomplete, create a review packet and assign no fictional reviewer.
  5. Hold: block the proposed action from producing a consequential effect while review is absent or conflict remains open.
  6. Receipt: preserve a machine- and human-readable record of the hold, its reasons, and the next required evidence.

What cannot happen here

The system cannot infer that the document authorizes the action, select a reviewer, resolve the conflict, or release an effect merely because a record exists. Those steps require separately lawful institutional action.
