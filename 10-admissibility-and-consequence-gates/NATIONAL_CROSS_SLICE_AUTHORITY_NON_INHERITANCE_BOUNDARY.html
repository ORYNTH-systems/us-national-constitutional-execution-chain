National Cross-Slice Authority Non-Inheritance Boundary

  • Evidence does not inherit authority when transferred between slices.
  • Authority does not inherit jurisdiction, delegation, status, finality, or consequence.
  • A decision in one slice is only an input candidate at another slice until the receiving gate admits it.
  • Shared actors, labels, dates, sources, or institutions do not collapse distinct predicates.
  • No interface may create a reviewer, decision, dispute disposition, remedy, hold, judgment, finality event, release, reopening, certification, officeholder, admission, legal effect, or governmental effect.
