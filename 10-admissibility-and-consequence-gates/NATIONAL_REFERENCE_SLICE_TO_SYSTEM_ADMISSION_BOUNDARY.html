National Reference Slice to System Admission Boundary

  • A frozen reference slice is evidence of framework state, not evidence that its proposed facts are true.
  • A slice digest proves byte-addressed integrity, not authority, jurisdiction, legal effect, or permission to execute.
  • Unresolved obligations cannot be collapsed, deduplicated across domains, silently inherited, or discharged by federation.
  • No national-system admission may occur without accountable human decision events and every predicate-specific gate.
  • Missing, conflicting, stale, superseded, or cross-domain-incompetent evidence remains fail-closed.
