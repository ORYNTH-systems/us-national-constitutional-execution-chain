National Cross-Branch Official Source Policy

Official-source status is publisher-, instrument-, actor-, jurisdiction-, process-, and time-specific. A bill does not prove presentment; a vote does not prove finality; a filing does not prove jurisdiction; an order does not prove service or current effect; and publication does not erase an open stay, appeal, conflict, correction, or superseding record.
