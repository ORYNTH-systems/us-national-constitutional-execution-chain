National Human-Decision Orchestration Contract

This contract routes each of the 360 unresolved cross-slice dependency obligations into an attributable human-review packet. Orchestration schedules and records review; it does not supply a reviewer, manufacture a decision, satisfy a predicate, transfer authority, or admit an effect.

RequirementMeaningFailure behavior
REVIEWER_IDENTITYA natural reviewer identity must be attributable and verifiable.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
REVIEWER_AUTHORITYThe reviewer must have a documented review mandate without acquiring substantive authority from the assignment.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
REVIEWER_INDEPENDENCEConflicts, dependencies, incentives, recusals, and separation requirements must be resolved.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
SOURCE_COMPLETENESSAll source-bound evidence and counterevidence required by the predicate must be present.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
SOURCE_INTEGRITYHashes, provenance, acquisition receipts, versions, and supersession status must be verified.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
CONFLICT_RESOLUTIONConflicting evidence and incompatible candidate routes must be expressly resolved or remain blocked.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
TEMPORAL_VALIDITYObservation, issuance, effective, expiration, challenge, and supersession times must be resolved.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
REASONED_FINDINGThe reviewer must record a bounded finding and reasons linked to the evidence packet.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
COUNTERFINDING_RESPONSEMaterial counterfindings must be accepted, rejected, or retained with reasons.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
DECISION_SCOPEThe decision must identify its exact predicate, interface, jurisdiction, scope, and prohibited extrapolations.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED
DECISION_SIGNATUREThe decision event must be signed, timestamped, immutable, and attributable.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED

EXECUTION_RECEIPTAny later admitted effect must link decision, authorization, execution, and outcome receipts.REQUIRED_FAIL_CLOSED

Prior unresolved national obligations remain 10,759. This batch creates no new substantive obligation and resolves none.
