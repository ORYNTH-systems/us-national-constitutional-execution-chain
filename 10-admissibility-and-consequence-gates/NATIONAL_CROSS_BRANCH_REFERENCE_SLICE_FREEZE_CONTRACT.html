National Cross-Branch Reference Slice Freeze Contract

This freeze binds the constitutional and execution structure, official-source pipeline, endpoint verification, immutable snapshots, source-bound proposals, normalization, semantic enrichment, evidence-review gates, unresolved obligations, and adversarial proofs to one content-addressed baseline.

The freeze proves preservation and internal consistency only. It does not resolve a packet, appoint a reviewer, create a decision, admit a fact, establish jurisdiction, authorize a remedy or hold, confer finality, release or reopen a matter, determine legal effect, or create governmental effect.

Mutation of any frozen input changes the aggregate digest and requires a new explicit expansion baseline.
