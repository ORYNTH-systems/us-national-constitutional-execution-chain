National Consequential Effect Admission and Hold Boundary

A source, observation, allegation, vote, filing, order, certification, announcement, technical capability, or urgent condition does not independently establish authority or downstream effect. Consequential execution requires an authenticated source, competent actor, jurisdiction, completed process, effective-time proof, conflict disposition, current finality state, effect scope, and an immutable receipt.

A hold preserves the prior admitted state. It does not create a decision, establish the merits, or erase provenance.
