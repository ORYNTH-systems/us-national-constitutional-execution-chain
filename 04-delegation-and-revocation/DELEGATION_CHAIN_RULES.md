# Delegation Chain Rules

Every transfer of operational authority must identify the delegator, delegate, authority basis, scope, conditions, start time, end time where applicable, and revocation path. A delegation may narrow but cannot enlarge what the delegator lawfully carries.

An acting role, contractor relationship, platform access grant, automated workflow, or organizational chart is not a substitute for a bounded delegation record.

Revocation, expiration, supersession, or unresolved scope blocks future consequence admissibility until the chain is re-established.
