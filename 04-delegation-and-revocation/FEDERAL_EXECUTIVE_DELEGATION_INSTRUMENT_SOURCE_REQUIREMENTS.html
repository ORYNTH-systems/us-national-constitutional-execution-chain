Federal Executive Delegation Instrument Source Requirements

A delegation candidate requires the authenticated instrument, competent delegator, delegate, source authority, delegated function, scope, conditions, signature or issuance evidence, effective time, expiry, revocation state, supersession state, and downstream subdelegation rule.

An organizational chart, job title, biography, capability, practice, press release, or unsigned summary cannot independently establish delegated authority.

Missing scope, missing time, missing source authority, unresolved revocation, or an invalid office chain blocks admission.
