Federal Executive Constitutional Delegation Boundary

Constitutional structure, office occupancy, statutory authority, delegation, subdelegation, operational capability, and execution effect are separate propositions.

A delegation cannot create power absent from its source, enlarge the delegator's scope, cure an invalid appointment, cross a jurisdiction boundary without authority, survive revocation or expiry, or transform technical capability into permission.

Every asserted delegation requires an identified delegator, delegate, source authority, subject scope, conditions, effective interval, revocation state, and an authenticated instrument. Missing or conflicting evidence blocks progression.
