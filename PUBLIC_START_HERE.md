# National Constitutional Execution Reference Framework

## Public entry point

This repository is a released **reference framework** for tracing public authority through a national constitutional system without allowing evidence, capability, automation, or institutional proximity to become authority by implication.

It is not a live governmental system, a register of current officeholders, a source of legal advice, or a mechanism that confers power. It is a reconstructible architecture for answering a prior question: **what must be true, shown, reviewed, and preserved before a public action may be treated as authoritative or consequential?**

### What v1.0.0 establishes

- Six reference slices: congressional, federal executive, federal judiciary, federalism, election/public participation, and national cross-branch checks/remedies/finality.
- Source-acquisition, parsing-proposal, normalization, conflict-routing, evidence-review, adversarial-proof, and freeze/release structures for each slice.
- A fail-closed rule: a missing reviewer, unresolved conflict, absent evidence, stale evidence, or missing lawful predicate does not become an inferred approval.
- A frozen, reconstructible release baseline, while preserving all unresolved human-review obligations as open.

### The non-negotiable boundary

The framework does **not** assert a current governmental fact merely because a source was collected; does not appoint reviewers; does not resolve conflicts; and does not produce a legal or governmental effect. In this framework, a source is not an admission, a proposal is not a decision, and a decision is not a consequence without the independently required gate.

### Release identity

`v1.0.0` is the complete, reconstructible, fail-closed national constitutional execution reference framework. It freezes 702 parent-state HTML artifacts and preserves 10,759 unresolved human-review obligations. Its release designation is a framework release—not a claim that governmental decisions, authority transfers, legal effects, or operational deployment have occurred.
