Federal Executive Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Availability is not authenticity of every field.
  • Official publication is not proof of current effective status.
  • Response bytes must be separately acquired, hashed, receipted, and parser-bound before extraction.
  • Cross-source conflict, effective time, appointment route, delegation scope, revocation, and judicial effect remain separately gated.
  • An unavailable official endpoint cannot be silently replaced by an unofficial source.
