Whistleblowing is an important service to the public and the government.

Whistleblowers perform an important public service when they report evidence of waste, fraud, abuse, and mismanagement. Federal laws protect whistleblowers from retaliation for reporting wrongdoing.

Oversight In Action

CIGIE’s Oversight in Action series highlights cross-cutting work and collaboration in the Inspector General community as we work to promote economy, efficiency, and effectiveness throughout the federal government.

Image

Pandemic Oversight

The Pandemic Response Accountability Committee (PRAC) was created to support and coordinate independent oversight of $5 trillion in pandemic relief programs and spending.

Image

Ukraine Oversight

After the start of Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022, more than 20 oversight entities across the Federal Government have worked together to deliver independent, whole-of-government oversight of U.S. assistance to Ukraine. This website, managed by the Special IG for OAR, brings that work together.
