National Cross-Branch Parsing and Proposal Contract

Parser version: NCEC-CROSS-BRANCH-HTML-LEXICAL-1.1. Inputs are the eighteen immutable, hash-verified Batch 98 snapshots. Extraction is lexical and source-bound.

  • Labels preserve observed text and structural locators.
  • Links preserve observed labels and raw href values.
  • A nonempty snapshot with no parser-selected text or anchor receives one explicit source-container review proposal; that record asserts parser insufficiency only and does not infer page content.
  • Signals are provisional lexical routes, not factual, legal, jurisdictional, remedial, or cross-branch determinations.
  • Duplicates, navigation noise, ambiguity, identity, event scope, effective time, presentment, vote, consent, jurisdiction, remedy, supersession, finality, release, reopening, and legal effect remain unresolved.
  • A discovery router does not substitute for the predicate-specific primary record of the competent institution.
  • Every record is PROPOSED_NOT_ADMITTED.
