Federalism Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Observed reachability does not establish authenticity of every field or document.
  • An official publisher or discovery router does not prove that a page describes the current legal or factual state.
  • Endpoint verification does not acquire an immutable canonical snapshot.
  • A federal source cannot silently establish a state, territorial, or tribal fact; a discovery router cannot substitute for the primary publisher.
  • Recognition, sovereignty, jurisdiction, immunity, preemption, treaty status, compact consent, boundaries, currentness, and legal effect remain unresolved.
  • Unavailable, amended, superseded, corrected, disputed, or withdrawn records remain explicit states and cannot be inferred.
  • An unavailable official source cannot be silently replaced by an unofficial source.

Decision effect: NONE.
