Federal Judiciary Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Observed reachability does not establish authenticity of every field or document.
  • An official publisher does not prove that a page describes the current legal or factual state.
  • Endpoint verification does not acquire an immutable canonical snapshot.
  • A docket listing does not prove jurisdiction, an opinion does not necessarily equal the judgment, and judgment entry does not prove finality or binding scope.
  • Sealed, redacted, restricted, unavailable, superseded, or corrected records remain explicit states and cannot be inferred.
  • An unavailable official source cannot be silently replaced by an unofficial source.

Decision effect: NONE.
