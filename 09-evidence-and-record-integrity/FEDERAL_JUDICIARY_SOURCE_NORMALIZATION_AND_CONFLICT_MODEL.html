Federal Judiciary Source Normalization and Conflict Model

Normalization preserves original bytes, snapshot hash, source locator, court and docket locator, observation time, document type, parser version, access restriction, and extracted text. It cannot erase disagreement, infer missing authority, or merge court, case, document, officer, order, judgment, and effect identities.
