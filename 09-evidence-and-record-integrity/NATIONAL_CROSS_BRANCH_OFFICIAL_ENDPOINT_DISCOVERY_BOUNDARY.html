National Cross-Branch Official Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Reachability proves transport observation only.
  • An official host does not establish authenticity, completeness, currentness, jurisdiction, actor capacity, effective time, finality, or legal effect for every artifact it serves.
  • Discovery routers identify competent publishers but do not replace predicate-specific primary records.
  • Endpoint verification does not create an immutable snapshot, parse facts, establish presentment, validate a vote or consent, admit a dispute, resolve a claim, issue a remedy, prove finality, authorize release, or reopen a matter.
  • Unavailable official sources remain explicit and cannot be silently replaced by projections, press reports, party statements, social posts, mirrors, or model inference.

Decision effect: NONE.
