National Reviewer Identity, Independence, and Accountability Boundary

  • No reviewer identity may be inferred, generated, inherited, or represented by software.
  • Assignment does not confer subject-matter authority, jurisdiction, office, delegation, or permission to create an effect.
  • Reviewer independence must be evidenced for the exact packet and decision event.
  • Automated tools may organize evidence but may not sign, decide, admit, authorize, or execute.
  • Recusal, disagreement, escalation, correction, challenge, and reopening remain preserved.
