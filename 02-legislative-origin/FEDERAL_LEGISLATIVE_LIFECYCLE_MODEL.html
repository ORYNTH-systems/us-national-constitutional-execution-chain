Federal Legislative Lifecycle Model

Private structural model. These states separate drafting, chamber procedure, bicameral passage, presentment, enactment, publication, codification, operability, and implementation. The model records legal and institutional transitions; it does not enact law or decide a disputed legal question.

Basis separation. CONSTITUTIONAL identifies an express constitutional entry point. CHAMBER_PROCEDURE and related procedural bases require current rules and official events. PUBLICATION_PRACTICE_AND_LAW, CODIFICATION_PRACTICE_AND_LAW, and STATUTORY_EXECUTION require their own legal sources. No lower-order basis is silently promoted into constitutional text.
