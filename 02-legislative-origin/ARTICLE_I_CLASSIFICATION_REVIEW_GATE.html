Article I Classification Review Gate

Private review control. No classification may enter an authority chain merely because it was generated, proposed, scored, or reviewed.

Admission gate

A proposal may enter review only when its parent, candidate, source span, label, rationale, dependencies, proposer, and time are present and internally consistent. Missing fields produce CHANGES_REQUIRED, not inference.

Acceptance gate

  1. The proposed label exists in the controlled vocabulary.
  2. The quoted span matches the preserved candidate text.
  3. The rationale does not exceed the quoted source.
  4. Conditions, exceptions, cross-references, and rights-impact flags are explicit.
  5. Contrary readings and material uncertainty are recorded.
  6. The reviewer is identified and distinct from any automated system that generated the proposal.
  7. The decision, rationale, timestamp, and prior state are preserved.

Disagreement handling

  • Material disagreement changes the state to DISPUTED.
  • Each position remains separately attributable and source-linked.
  • No majority count, confidence score, or model output resolves a constitutional disagreement by itself.
  • Resolution requires a recorded review decision and may still preserve minority or alternative readings.

Fail-closed rules

  • Unknown source, missing source, or source mismatch: block.
  • Unknown label or altered vocabulary: block.
  • Unresolved dependency, exception, conflict, or rights impact: block.
  • Missing reviewer identity, rationale, state transition, or timestamp: block.
  • Automated proposal without responsible human review: block.
  • Stale, superseded, rejected, or disputed record: block.
  • Attempt to treat analytical acceptance as legal authority: block.

Permitted output of this gate

The highest state produced here is ACCEPTED_ANALYTICAL. Any later claim that a constitutional unit supports governmental action must pass a separate authority-linking process covering current law, office, jurisdiction, delegation, procedure, evidence, effect, and remedy.
