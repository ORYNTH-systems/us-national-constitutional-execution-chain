Article I Classification Proposal Contract

Private analytical contract. A proposal record is an attributable claim about a source candidate. It is not constitutional text, accepted interpretation, legal authority, or governmental effect.

Identity and immutability

A proposal identifier binds one candidate, one label, one rationale version, and one source hash. Changing any bound value creates a new proposal version; it does not silently mutate the prior record.

Multiplicity

A candidate may have multiple proposals. Each proposed label remains an independent record with independent rationale, dependencies, review state, and history.

Prohibited claims

  • A proposal must not state that it is the Constitution.
  • A proposal must not state that acceptance creates legal authority.
  • A proposal must not erase alternative or rejected readings.
  • A model confidence score must not substitute for review.
