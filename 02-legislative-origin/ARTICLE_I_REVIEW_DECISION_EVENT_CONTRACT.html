Article I Review Decision Event Contract

Private analytical decision contract. A conforming event records review of one proposal version. It cannot create governmental authority.

Required event fields

FieldRequirement
decision_event_idUnique immutable event identifier.
proposal_idExact proposal identifier.
proposal_sha256Hash binding to the reviewed proposal representation.
reviewer_idIdentifier admitted under the reviewer boundary.
independence_classDeclared reviewer independence class.
prior_stateState immediately before the decision.
decisionACCEPT_ANALYTICAL, REJECT, CHANGES_REQUIRED, DISPUTE, or REMAIN_UNRESOLVED.
findingSource-linked reasoning addressing label fit, overreach, dependencies, exceptions, and competing readings.
rights_findingResolved finding or an express unresolved block.
dependency_findingResolved dependency record or an express unresolved block.
decided_atUTC timestamp.
signatureReviewer authentication or signed attestation reference.

Decision effects

  • ACCEPT_ANALYTICAL permits only repository analytical acceptance.
  • REJECT preserves the rejected proposal and rationale.
  • CHANGES_REQUIRED requires a new proposal version.
  • DISPUTE preserves competing positions and blocks acceptance.
  • REMAIN_UNRESOLVED keeps downstream authority use blocked.

Fail-closed rule

An absent, incomplete, self-generated automated, unsigned, source-mismatched, conflicted, or internally inconsistent decision event has no state-changing effect.
