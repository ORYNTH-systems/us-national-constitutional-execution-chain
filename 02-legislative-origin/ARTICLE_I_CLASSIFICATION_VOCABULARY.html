Article I Classification Vocabulary

Private analytical vocabulary. These labels describe candidate textual functions for review. They do not determine constitutional meaning, create authority, resolve a legal dispute, or replace controlling law.

Separation rules

  • Source text is not interpretation.
  • A punctuation candidate is not a legal clause.
  • A classification proposal is not an accepted classification.
  • An accepted analytical classification is not governmental authority.
  • Confidence is not validity.
  • Silence or missing evidence is not permission.

Permitted candidate labels

CodeLabelPermitted analytical useExclusion
STRUCTUREInstitutional structureCandidate text appears to establish or relate constitutional institutions, bodies, offices, or composition.Does not prove an institution may take a particular action.
VESTINGVesting languageCandidate text appears to place a named power in a named constitutional body.Does not determine the full scope or exclusivity of that power.
POWEREnumerated powerCandidate text appears to identify an affirmative governmental power.Does not prove that a proposed exercise satisfies every limit or predicate.
DUTYMandatory dutyCandidate text appears to require conduct from a constitutional actor.Does not decide enforceability, remedy, or responsible implementing office.
PROHIBITIONProhibition or limitCandidate text appears to prohibit, withhold, or limit governmental action.Does not settle exceptions, incorporation, standing, or remedy.
QUALIFICATIONQualificationCandidate text appears to set eligibility or status requirements.Does not establish that a particular person satisfies them.
PROCEDURERequired procedureCandidate text appears to prescribe a process, vote, sequence, record, or institutional act.Does not prove the procedure occurred in a particular case.
CONDITIONCondition or predicateCandidate text appears to make authority or effect dependent on a stated condition.Does not establish that the condition is presently satisfied.
EXCEPTIONExceptionCandidate text appears to qualify an otherwise applicable statement.Must not be expanded beyond its source-supported scope.
TEMPORALTemporal ruleCandidate text appears to establish timing, duration, frequency, deadline, or term.Does not prove current activation or continuing validity.
ACCOUNTABILITYAccountability mechanismCandidate text appears to establish removal, discipline, record, disclosure, or answerability.Does not itself execute accountability or determine liability.
INTERBRANCHInterbranch relationCandidate text appears to connect powers or acts across constitutional bodies.Does not erase each body's independent predicates and limits.
FEDERALISMFederal-state relationCandidate text appears to allocate, preserve, constrain, or coordinate federal and state roles.Does not decide preemption or a specific jurisdictional conflict.
RIGHTS_IMPACTRights-impact flagCandidate text may require a separate rights and liberties review before operational use.It is a review trigger, not a conclusion that a right is granted, denied, or violated.
CROSS_REFERENCECross-reference dependencyCandidate text cannot be responsibly assessed without another constitutional unit or source.Must not be classified in isolation as complete authority.
UNRESOLVEDUnresolvedThe current record does not support a stable classification.Must remain blocked from downstream authority claims.

Cardinality

A candidate may receive zero, one, or multiple proposed labels. Multiple labels must remain separately justified. UNRESOLVED may coexist with proposed labels but prevents acceptance and downstream use.

Required evidence for a proposal

  1. Exact parent section identifier.
  2. Exact punctuation-candidate identifier.
  3. Verbatim source span from the private derived HTML record.
  4. Proposed label code.
  5. Source-bound rationale limited to the text being classified.
  6. Dependencies, exceptions, conflicts, and unresolved questions.
  7. Proposer identity and timestamp.
