Forbidden transitions

  • UNCLASSIFIED directly to ACCEPTED_ANALYTICAL.
  • PROPOSED directly to governmental authority.
  • DISPUTED directly to accepted without renewed review.
  • REJECTED back to active by mutation; a new version is required.
  • Any transition without actor, timestamp, prior state, new state, and rationale.

Downstream rule

Even ACCEPTED_ANALYTICAL remains blocked from governmental effect until later authority-linking, jurisdiction, delegation, procedure, evidence, execution, and remedy gates are independently satisfied.
