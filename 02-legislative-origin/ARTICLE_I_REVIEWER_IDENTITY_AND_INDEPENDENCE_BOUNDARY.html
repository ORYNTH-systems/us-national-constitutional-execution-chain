Article I Reviewer Identity and Independence Boundary

Private governance control. A reviewer evaluates repository proposals. A reviewer does not become a constitutional authority, court, legislature, executive officer, or source of law by participating.

Required reviewer identity record

  • Stable reviewer identifier and display name.
  • Affiliation and relevant role.
  • Declared subject-matter competence.
  • Declared conflicts, interests, prior authorship, and dependencies.
  • Review scope and assignment timestamp.
  • Authentication or signature method used for the decision event.

Independence classes

ClassMeaningPermitted decision effect
AUTHOR_REVIEWReviewer authored or directed the proposal or generating rule.May identify defects and recommend disposition; cannot alone establish independent confirmation.
INTERNAL_INDEPENDENT_REVIEWReviewer is within the same project but did not generate the proposal.May support analytical acceptance when conflicts are disclosed.
EXTERNAL_SUBJECT_REVIEWReviewer is outside the project and has relevant constitutional or legal competence.May support analytical acceptance; does not create legal authority.
AUTOMATED_ASSISTANCESoftware or a model performs checking or proposes findings.Advisory only; cannot issue the final review decision.

Separation requirements

  • Proposal generation and final decision identities remain separately recorded.
  • Automated assistance is disclosed by system and method.
  • Conflict disclosure is mandatory and cannot be replaced by confidence scoring.
  • Missing identity, competence, scope, conflict disclosure, or signature blocks the decision.
