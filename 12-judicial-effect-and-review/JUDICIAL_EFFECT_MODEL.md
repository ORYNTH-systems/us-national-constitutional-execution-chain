# Judicial Effect and Review Model

A court filing, argument, order, opinion, judgment, stay, injunction, or mandate has a distinct record identity and effect. The repository must not collapse them into the assertion that "a court decided" something broader than the actual record supports.

`case/controversy record -> court and decision-maker -> record before court -> disposition -> stated effect and scope -> stay/finality status -> review path`

Judicial review can test governmental action, but a judicial record does not automatically erase source, jurisdiction, evidence, timing, or remedy requirements elsewhere in the chain. The effect asserted for a judicial action must identify whom it concerns, what it reaches, when it begins, what remains unresolved, and the route for further review.
