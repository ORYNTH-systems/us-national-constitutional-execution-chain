Federal Judiciary Case, Order, and Judgment Source Requirements

Case identity, filing, jurisdiction, order, judgment, appeal, mandate, finality, remedy, and enforcement are separate evidence objects. A docket listing does not prove jurisdiction; an opinion does not necessarily equal the judgment; judgment entry does not by itself prove finality; and finality does not establish universal scope or an authorized enforcement actor.
