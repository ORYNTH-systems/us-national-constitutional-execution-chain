Federal Judiciary Finality, Enforcement, and Remedy Boundary

Judgment entry, finality, appellate exhaustion, mandate, binding scope, remedy, and enforcement are separate evidence-bound determinations. A judgment is not automatically final; a final judgment is not automatically universal; a remedy is not automatically authorized; and an enforceable obligation does not identify an authorized enforcing actor.
