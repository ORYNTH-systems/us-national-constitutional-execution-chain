# High-Impact Execution Boundary

High impact is a recordable effect category, not a claim that a system is the responsible actor. The responsible public principal remains identifiable at every effect gate.

An effect is not eligible for execution when any required authority, delegation, jurisdiction, scope, temporal status, predicate, evidence, review, or remedy link is missing, conflicting, expired, or outside its stated limit. The prescribed response is to block, pause, or route for authorized review; it is never to infer missing authority from urgency, confidence, model performance, or institutional affiliation.

Every completed action produces an effect receipt stating the acting principal, system participation, governing authority records, decision or approval record, actual scope, timing, and the available challenge or correction route.
