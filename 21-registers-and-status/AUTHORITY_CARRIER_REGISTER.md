# Authority Carrier Register

| Family | Prefix | Domain | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office Status Record | `OSR-` | 01 | Time-bound office or role status |
| Appointment Event Record | `AER-` | 06 | Appointment, confirmation, acting, vacancy, and succession events |
| Delegation Record | `DLR-` | 04 | Bounded authority transfer |
| Revocation Event Record | `RER-` | 04 | Revocation, expiry, suspension, or supersession |
| Jurisdiction Record | `JUR-` | 05 | Actor/action-specific jurisdiction boundary |
| Federalism Interface Record | `FIR-` | 05 | Cross-sovereign boundary and conflict path |

Batch 03 supplies record families and block conditions only. It does not populate officeholders or conclude that any individual action is lawful.
