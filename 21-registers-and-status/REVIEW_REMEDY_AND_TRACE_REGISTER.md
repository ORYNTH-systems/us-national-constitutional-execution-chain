# Review, Remedy, and Trace Register

| Family | Prefix | Domain | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Judicial Effect Record | `JER-` | 12 | Bounded judicial filing, order, judgment, stay, or mandate effect |
| Remedy Event Record | `RMR-` | 17 | Challenge, pause, correction, reversal, or closure event |
| Public Trace Record | `PTR-` | 18 | Public-safe reconstruction of a consequential action |

Batch 06 establishes these record families and stop conditions. It does not resolve a case, grant a remedy, disclose protected material, or create a legal effect.
