# Evidence and Effect Register

| Family | Prefix | Domain | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence Record | `EVR-` | 09 | Captured evidence with provenance and limitation state |
| Predicate Assessment Record | `PAR-` | 09 | Current state of a required factual or legal predicate |
| Consequence Gate Record | `CGR-` | 10 | Declared complete/block/escalate decision before effect |
| Effect Receipt Record | `EFR-` | 11 | Durable observed effect and review path |

Batch 05 establishes no facts and authorizes no effect. It defines the conditions required before an action can represent itself as complete enough for consequence.
