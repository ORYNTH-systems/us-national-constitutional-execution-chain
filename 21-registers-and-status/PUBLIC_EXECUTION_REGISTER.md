# Public Execution Register

| Family | Prefix | Domain | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public Money Record | `PMR-` | 07 | Appropriation through payment and closeout |
| Outcome Receipt Record | `POR-` | 07 | Evidence of a claimed funded outcome |
| Administrative Action Record | `AAR-` | 08 | Rule, guidance, order, waiver, or enforcement path |
| External Execution Record | `EER-` | 15 | Bounded contractor or other external execution |

Batch 04 creates no program, appropriation, rule, contract, payment, or external authority. It supplies the evidence and boundary requirements required to examine them.
