# Governance

## Purpose

This repository publishes a constitutional execution reference framework. Its governance exists to preserve attribution, provenance, reviewability, and the boundary between a reference artifact and a live governmental act.

## Publisher and stewardship

ORYNTH Systems is the repository publisher. Repository stewardship concerns the integrity, scope, versioning, and public presentation of this work. Stewardship does not confer governmental authority, appoint public officials, decide legal questions, or convert repository content into governmental fact.

## Change classes

- **Editorial and navigation changes:** may clarify access without changing a framework claim or frozen artifact.
- **Additive reference changes:** may add orientation, adoption, or implementation-boundary material; they require a recorded conformance statement.
- **Framework changes:** may add or revise contracts, evidence models, proofs, or interfaces; they require versioned review, integrity documentation, and a new release decision.
- **Frozen-release artifacts:** may not be silently modified. A correction requires a separately versioned supersession or migration record.

## Decision discipline

No contributor, automated process, source snapshot, or proposal becomes self-authorizing. Changes must preserve the framework's non-operational boundary, provenance, and fail-closed conditions.

## Public participation

Issues and pull requests may identify errors, ambiguities, missing sources, usability problems, or implementation questions. They are proposals for review, not decisions or admissions.
