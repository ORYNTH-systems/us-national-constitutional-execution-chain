Congressional Registry Admission and Revocation Rules

Private fail-closed controls. These rules govern registry claims. They do not choose members, officers, committees, rules, or governmental outcomes.

IDTopicRequirementFailure state
INST-RULE-001IdentityEvery institution, body, seat, office, role, person relation, rule, and delegation receives a stable nonreused identifier.Reject admission
INST-RULE-002Official sourceCurrent-status claims require an attributable official source snapshot and content hash.SOURCE_PENDING
INST-RULE-003BitemporalityRecord both valid time in the institution and transaction time in this registry.Block current-state claim
INST-RULE-004IntervalStart, end, open-end meaning, and timezone or date convention must be explicit.UNKNOWN
INST-RULE-005No overlapA seat or exclusive office cannot have overlapping ACTIVE tenures unless the governing structure expressly permits it.CONFLICTED
INST-RULE-006Selection provenanceElection, appointment, chamber choice, designation, succession, or acting route must be identified and evidenced.Block holder authority
INST-RULE-007QualificationRequired qualification and chamber judgment status remain separate from election or appointment evidence.CHALLENGED or UNKNOWN
INST-RULE-008Committee derivationCommittee existence and power require a current chamber rule, resolution, statute where applicable, or other official creating source.Block committee attribution
INST-RULE-009MembershipCommittee membership requires member tenure plus a valid appointment or assignment event for the same interval.Block committee vote attribution
INST-RULE-010JurisdictionSubject jurisdiction must preserve inclusions, exclusions, overlaps, referrals, and conflicts.Block exclusive-jurisdiction claim
INST-RULE-011DelegationDelegation must identify source authority, parties, scope, conditions, duration, subdelegation, and revocation.Block delegated act
INST-RULE-012Remaining scopeEach use of delegated authority is checked against the authority remaining at that event time.Block exceeded or stale act
INST-RULE-013VacancyVacancy never silently transfers all functions; the authorized filling, succession, or acting route must be proven.No holder authority
INST-RULE-014RevocationRevocation terminates prospective authority at its effective time while preserving prior events and challenges.Block post-revocation act
INST-RULE-015CorrectionCorrections are append-only provenance events and never rewrite historical evidence invisibly.Reject correction
INST-RULE-016ConflictConflicting official sources are retained, prioritized only by an explicit rule, and routed for authorized resolution.CONFLICTED
INST-RULE-017FreshnessEach record carries retrieved-at, observed-effective interval, and freshness policy; age alone does not prove invalidity.SOURCE_PENDING or UNKNOWN
INST-RULE-018AutomationAutomated systems may collect, compare, hash, route, and flag; they may not appoint, elect, qualify, delegate, vote, revoke, or self-certify public authority.Block and record stop event
INST-RULE-019Person separationPerson identity, seat, office, role, tenure, delegation, and act remain separate joinable objects.Reject collapsed record
INST-RULE-020Effect boundaryRegistry admission supports traceability only and does not itself create office, membership, jurisdiction, delegation, or governmental effect.Reject effect claim
