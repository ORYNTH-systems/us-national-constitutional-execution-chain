Congressional Temporal Adversarial Test Contract

Each test injects one invalid or insufficient temporal condition into a copy of a review-packet evaluation context. The protected decision ledger is never used as test storage and is never mutated.

The only passing outcome is BLOCK_NO_RESOLUTION. A test cannot establish truth, identity, office, membership, vacancy, leadership status, an effective interval, constitutional authority, or governmental effect.

Unexpected acceptance, implicit defaulting, source-count voting, reviewer fabrication, or direct ledger mutation fails the batch.
