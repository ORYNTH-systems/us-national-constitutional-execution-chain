Congressional Source Normalization and Conflict Model

Private fail-closed model. Normalization never overwrites source values. Conflicts remain explicit objects until an authorized resolution or correction event exists.

IDConflict kindDetectionResponse
SRC-CONFLICT-001IDENTITY_COLLISIONSame name maps to multiple plausible people, offices, seats, bodies, or identifiers.Do not merge; require distinguishing official evidence.
SRC-CONFLICT-002OVERLAPPING_EXCLUSIVE_TENURETwo ACTIVE occupants overlap for the same exclusive seat or office.Mark CONFLICTED; block holder-dependent acts.
SRC-CONFLICT-003INTERVAL_DISAGREEMENTSources disagree on start, end, effective date, or transaction sequence.Preserve all claims; route temporal adjudication.
SRC-CONFLICT-004ROLE_DISAGREEMENTSources disagree on office, committee assignment, leadership, or acting status.Block role claim until authoritative resolution.
SRC-CONFLICT-005JURISDICTION_DISAGREEMENTRules or official records conflict about committee or office scope.Retain overlap and dispute; block exclusive-scope inference.
SRC-CONFLICT-006RULE_VERSION_DISAGREEMENTCurrent governing rule version or effective interval is unclear.Block procedure-dependent admission.
SRC-CONFLICT-007TEXT_HASH_MISMATCHObjects claimed as the same official representation have different bytes.Quarantine; establish edition, correction, or mutation provenance.
SRC-CONFLICT-008PUBLISHER_SCOPE_EXCEEDEDA source is used to establish a fact outside its official scope.Reject unsupported field while retaining the snapshot.
SRC-CONFLICT-009STALE_CURRENT_CLAIMRecord exceeds class-specific freshness policy without corroborating continuation evidence.Mark freshness unresolved; do not infer termination or continuation.
SRC-CONFLICT-010CORRECTION_WITHOUT_PROVENANCEA changed value lacks an authorized correction or supersession trail.Reject overwrite and preserve both versions for review.
SRC-CONFLICT-011PARSER_DIVERGENCEParser versions derive materially different records from identical bytes.Quarantine derived records and preserve both parser receipts.
SRC-CONFLICT-012SOURCE_UNAVAILABLEOfficial source cannot be retrieved or verified.Record acquisition failure; retain last admitted state with explicit freshness, never fabricate replacement.

Non-equivalences

  • Retrieved is not authenticated.
  • Authenticated is not current.
  • Current is not universally authoritative.
  • Name match is not identity match.
  • Normalization is not source correction.
  • Higher-ranked source is not permission to erase conflicting evidence.
  • Stale is not automatically false, terminated, or superseded.
  • Registry admission is not governmental appointment or authority.
