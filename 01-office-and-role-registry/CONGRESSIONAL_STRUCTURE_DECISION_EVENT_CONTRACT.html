Congressional Structure Decision Event Contract

Every decision is append-only and must include the proposal ID, prior state, proposed next state, reviewer ID, reviewer-role evidence, independence attestation hash, source and packet hashes, rationale, timestamp, signature evidence, concurrence state, and supersession pointer where applicable.

Permitted decisions

  • ACCEPT_FOR_ADMISSION_REVIEW
  • REJECT_PROPOSAL
  • REQUEST_MORE_EVIDENCE
  • DEFER_DECISION
  • DISPUTE_CLASSIFICATION
  • MERGE_DUPLICATE_CANDIDATES
  • SPLIT_COMPOUND_CANDIDATE
  • SUPERSEDE_PRIOR_DECISION

No single review event directly creates an admitted institution, person, membership, jurisdiction, authority, or governmental effect. Admission requires a later, separately proven transition.
