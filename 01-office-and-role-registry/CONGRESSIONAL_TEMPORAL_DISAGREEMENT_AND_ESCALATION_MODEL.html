Congressional Temporal Disagreement and Escalation Model

  1. Preserve every source-bound candidate and its hash.
  2. Record agreement, disagreement, absence, staleness, and interval overlap separately.
  3. Require corroboration for a single-source claim.
  4. Escalate conflicting dates, Congress numbers, vacancy signals, succession claims, or leadership intervals to an independent second review.
  5. Retain CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED when the evidence does not support a bounded disposition.

No majority rule, confidence score, or source count automatically creates a resolution.
