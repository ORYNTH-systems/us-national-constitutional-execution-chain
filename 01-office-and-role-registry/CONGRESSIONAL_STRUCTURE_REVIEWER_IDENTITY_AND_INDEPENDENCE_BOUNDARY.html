Congressional Structure Reviewer Identity and Independence Boundary

Required identity evidence

  1. Stable reviewer identifier.
  2. Named reviewer role and competence basis.
  3. Timestamped conflict-of-interest disclosure.
  4. Independence attestation tied to the review assignment.
  5. Authentication or signature evidence for every decision event.

Disqualifying conditions

  • Anonymous, synthetic, unverifiable, or self-asserted reviewer identity.
  • Undisclosed authorship, financial, organizational, familial, political, or litigation interest.
  • Review of one own proposal without independent concurrence.
  • Missing source access, missing competence basis, or expired attestation.

Machine assistance may organize evidence but may not satisfy the human-review requirement or create authority.

Default state: AWAITING_QUALIFIED_REVIEWER. Decision effect: NONE.
