Congressional Proposal Resolution Rules

  1. Resolution dossiers reorganize existing evidence; they do not create new facts.
  2. Observed labels, hrefs, snapshots, evidence hashes, and packet hashes remain immutable.
  3. Within-class duplication and cross-class overlap remain distinct review questions.
  4. A reviewer must address the paired counterfinding before selecting merge, split, retain, reject, request-evidence, or defer.
  5. Machine-generated grouping may be corrected only by an append-only signed review event.
  6. Unresolved clusters remain fail-closed and unadmitted.

Decision effect: NONE.
