Congressional Parser and Pagination Boundary

  • Parser output is derived evidence, never canonical source.
  • Every parser has a stable ID, version, expected content type, structural assumptions, field map, warning set, and rejection conditions.
  • Pagination completion requires recorded first page, continuation rule, every page receipt, termination predicate, duplicate detection, and gap test.
  • A next link, offset, page number, date cursor, Congress/session key, or sitemap boundary is followed only when permitted by the endpoint profile.
  • Rate limiting causes a resumable stop, not skipped pages.
  • Schema or DOM drift quarantines affected output until the parser is reviewed.
  • Partial acquisition is labeled partial and cannot be represented as complete.
  • Retries receive new attempt receipts and never replace failed-attempt evidence.
  • Cross-source joins occur only after stable identity resolution.
