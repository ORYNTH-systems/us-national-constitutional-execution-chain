# Office and Role Admission Rules

An office or role entry answers only who may carry a defined institutional function; it does not presume authority for a particular effect.

An entry requires an identified office or role, institutional location, lawful-source reference, current-status basis, and time boundary. Where appointment, election, acting status, confirmation, vacancy, removal, or succession is material, the corresponding official record must be linked before consequence admissibility.

No name, title, or institutional affiliation is accepted as a self-proving authority claim.
