Congressional Reference Slice Freeze Contract

This freeze identifies an immutable, hash-addressed engineering baseline. It does not declare the congressional system legally complete, resolve human review queues, admit institutional facts, or create governmental authority.

Any later change must occur in a new commit, preserve provenance to this baseline, identify superseded artifacts, and rerun applicable conservation, conflict, temporal, and adversarial proofs.

The baseline may support parallel expansion into other national execution-chain layers while its explicitly registered congressional human-review obligations remain unresolved.
