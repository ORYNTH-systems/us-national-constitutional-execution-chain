Congressional Temporal Decision Event Contract

A temporal decision event is a private review record, not constitutional text, law, an appointment, a membership determination, or governmental action.

Required fields

  • packet identifier and source candidate identifiers;
  • verified reviewer identity and independence state;
  • decision state, rationale, evidence references, and source hashes;
  • observation time, claimed effective interval, and uncertainty statement;
  • conflict disposition and a second review when escalation is required.

Permitted decision states

CONFIRMED_FOR_INTERVAL, REJECTED_AS_UNSUPPORTED, SUPERSEDED, CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED, or ABSTAINED.

Fail-closed rule

Missing identity, independence, evidence, source hash, effective interval, rationale, or required concurrence produces no decision event and no downstream admission.
