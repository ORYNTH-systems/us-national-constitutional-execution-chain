Congressional Structure Wave 01 Admission Boundary

  • These snapshots preserve official institutional-structure surfaces observed at one transaction time.
  • Names, memberships, leadership, assignments, jurisdictions, rules, dates, and links appearing inside them remain unparsed and unadmitted.
  • No snapshot independently establishes current office, tenure, delegation, authority, validity, or legal effect.
  • Parsing requires a versioned parser and source-location receipts.
  • Normalization requires original-value preservation.
  • Admission requires identity, time, corroboration, conflict, and review gates.
  • Later source changes create new snapshots and never overwrite these bytes.

Decision effect: NONE.
