# Office Status Lifecycle

An authority-bearing action must link to a current, identifiable office or role. The question is not merely whether an institution exists, but whether the actor carried the particular role at effect time.

`NOMINATED -> SELECTED/APPOINTED -> CONFIRMED where required -> ACTIVE -> VACANT, EXPIRED, REMOVED, RESIGNED, SUPERSEDED, or CONTESTED`

An acting or delegated role retains its distinct basis and time boundary. A title, organizational chart, credential, or past employment cannot substitute for current office status. Unknown, expired, removed, vacant, or materially contested status blocks consequence admissibility.
