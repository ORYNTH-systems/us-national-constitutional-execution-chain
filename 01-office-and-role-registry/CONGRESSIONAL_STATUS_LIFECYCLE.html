Congressional Status Lifecycle

Private temporal vocabulary. Status is always bound to an object, scope, valid interval, transaction time, and evidence set. ACTIVE never means universally authorized.

Transition controls

  • Only evidence-complete records may enter ACTIVE or ACTING.
  • A vacancy ends holder authority but does not erase the seat or office.
  • Expiration, revocation, supersession, and termination are distinct endings.
  • A material challenge or conflict travels with every dependent record.
  • No status transition deletes prior valid-time or transaction-time history.
