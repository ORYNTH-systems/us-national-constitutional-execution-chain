Congressional Structure Proposal Parser Rules

  1. Inputs are the five byte-preserved Batch 37 snapshots whose hashes resolve in the acquisition receipts.
  2. Extraction is limited to visible anchor labels and href values.
  3. Body candidates require institutional vocabulary and a committee, commission, House, or Senate link surface.
  4. Structural links are observations of source-page linkage only; they do not assert legal hierarchy, jurisdiction, membership, or authority.
  5. Rule-source links require a rule/procedure source page and rule-related label or URI evidence.
  6. People, officeholders, memberships, party data, contact data, and biographical claims are excluded.
  7. Every output remains PROPOSED_NOT_ADMITTED.

Decision effect: NONE.
