Congressional Temporal Evidence and Staleness Rules

  1. Retrieval time proves observation only.
  2. Publication, update, effective, beginning, and ending times are distinct.
  3. Congress number and term require explicit evidence.
  4. A current webpage does not prove uninterrupted incumbency.
  5. Vacancy requires an affected seat and supported start; an end remains open until supported.
  6. Succession requires predecessor, successor, and non-overlapping effective intervals or an unresolved conflict.
  7. Conflicting intervals block admission.
  8. Undated or stale evidence routes to review and cannot establish current status.
  9. Future-dated evidence cannot establish present effectiveness.
  10. Decision effect remains NONE.
