Congressional Effective-Time and Term-Context Model

Congress number
The numbered constitutional legislative period; unknown until supported by source evidence.
Term context
The bounded service or institutional interval associated with a proposition.
Observation time
When bytes were retrieved or an assertion was observed.
Effective time
When a status legally or institutionally began or ended; never inferred solely from observation time.
Vacancy interval
The supported interval during which a seat is vacant.
Succession interval
The transition between preceding and succeeding holders or statuses.
Staleness
The condition in which evidence cannot prove status at the requested effective time.

All temporal conclusions fail closed when evidence is missing, conflicting, ambiguous, or stale.
