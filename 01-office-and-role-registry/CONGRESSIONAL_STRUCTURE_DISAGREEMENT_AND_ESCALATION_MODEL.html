Congressional Structure Disagreement and Escalation Model

  1. Preserve each signed finding; never overwrite disagreement.
  2. Route disagreement to independent concurrence review.
  3. Require the concurrence reviewer to address both the finding and counterfinding.
  4. Keep the proposal fail-closed while evidence, identity, independence, or classification remains unresolved.
  5. Record recusal, replacement, remand, and supersession as new events.
  6. Never resolve disagreement by majority count alone when a constitutional-source or provenance defect remains.

Unresolved state: DISPUTED_NOT_ADMITTED. Decision effect: NONE.
