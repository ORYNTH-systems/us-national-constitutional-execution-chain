Congressional Official Source Policy

Private evidence policy. Source rank determines permitted evidentiary use, not truth by label. Every claim remains scoped to publisher authority, document type, covered interval, exact bytes, and conflicts.

IDRankSource classPermitted useBoundary
SRC-TIER-0011Controlling constitutional sourceConstitutional institution, office, term, qualification, chamber, vote, and presentment entry pointsDoes not identify current occupants or prove completion of a current event.
SRC-TIER-0022Official chamber rule, resolution, journal, clerk, secretary, or committee publicationCurrent chamber structure, rules, proceedings, membership, officers, committees, assignments, votes, and status eventsAuthority and coverage are specific to publisher, document type, chamber, Congress, and interval.
SRC-TIER-0033Official legislative information or authenticated publication serviceMeasures, actions, text versions, reports, public and private laws, calendars, and legislative metadataPresentation fields and update timing must be checked against underlying official records.
SRC-TIER-0044Official federal archival or records publicationPreserved historical records, authenticated enactments, statutes, regulations, and provenanceHistorical custody does not prove present office occupancy or current operative status by itself.
SRC-TIER-0055Official member, committee, or institutional pageCorroborating current identity, role, contact, assignment, publication, and announcement dataSelf-published page is scoped, may lag, and does not override a controlling chamber record.
SRC-TIER-0066Official state election, appointment, or vacancy recordFederal member election, appointment, vacancy, and state-interface evidenceMust be joined to the receiving chamber qualification and membership record.
SRC-TIER-0077Secondary referenceDiscovery, cross-checking, and anomaly detection onlyCannot independently admit a current institutional or authority claim.

Secondary sources may discover discrepancies but cannot independently admit current public-office or institutional status.
