Congressional Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Endpoint verification proves transport reachability, final-host conformity, a response status, and observed-byte identity at one transaction time.
  • It does not prove that a response is complete, current, legally controlling, internally consistent, or sufficient to admit a person, office, role, vote, rule, delegation, or legislative act.
  • Actual acquisition must preserve exact source bytes and a retrieval receipt before parsing.
  • API keys, pagination, rate limits, bulk-data conventions, machine-readable formats, corrections, and update cadence require endpoint-specific profiles.
  • Failures remain visible and never authorize substitution with an unofficial source.
  • No automated system may convert endpoint availability into public authority or governmental effect.

Decision effect: NONE.
