National Constitutional Execution Reference Framework: Origin and Authorship

Author: Ashley S. Harris, independent systems researcher; founder and principal, ORYNTH Systems.

Origin question: How can a public claim of authority be examined without treating institutional presence, a label, an available capability, or a successful action as proof of lawful authority?

Method: Preserve distinctions among source, interpretation, evidence, authority, proposal, admission, decision, execution, effect, provenance, challenge, and remedy.

Public boundary: This is independent, non-operational reference infrastructure. It neither represents a government institution nor creates authority, findings, legal effects, or governmental effects.
