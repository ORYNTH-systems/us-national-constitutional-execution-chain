National Reference Slice Federation Contract

This federation binds six independently frozen constitutional-execution reference slices into a national registry while preserving each slice identity, freeze commit, inventory, aggregate digest, unresolved-obligation boundary, and expansion handoff.

Federation is composition, not merger. No slice may borrow another slice's authority, evidence competence, reviewer, jurisdiction, decision, finality, or effect. A valid cross-slice claim must independently satisfy every governing interface and admission gate.
