Institutional applicability matrix

SettingBounded first useRequired institutional inputsRequired stop condition
Congressional processTrace a proposed source-to-role or source-to-term review path.Official source policy, temporal rule, reviewer assignment, conflict route.Hold if source, time context, or independent review is missing.
Executive administrationMap one appointment, delegation, rulemaking, or public-record workflow.Lawful authority predicate, record custodian, delegation boundary, evidence standard.Hold if appointment, delegation, or effective-time support is unresolved.
Judicial administrationModel the distinction among case record, order, judgment, remedy, and finality.Authorized record source, court-specific rule, review role, supersession route.Hold if jurisdiction, order status, or effect is unsupported.
Federalism interfaceMap one federal-state, territorial, tribal, or local interface without deciding its merits.Applicable source set, relationship boundary, conflict model, accountable reviewers.Hold if recognition, preemption, immunity, compact, or treaty status is not established.
Election administrationModel a non-live evidence and custody review path.Election-specific law, chain-of-custody evidence, reviewer independence, challenge path.Hold if eligibility, custody, canvass, certification, or contest requirements are unresolved.
Cross-branch remedyMap a stay, challenge, correction, reversal, release, or reopening pathway.Trigger source, governing authority, effect boundary, remedy/finality rule.Hold if a remedy or finality predicate is absent, contested, or superseded.

This matrix is an orientation tool. It neither identifies a current institution nor determines a current legal outcome.
