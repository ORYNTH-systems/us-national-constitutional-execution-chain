# Election and Certification Integrity Model

This domain records the legally defined lifecycle of an election-related public act without deciding any election, candidate, contest, outcome, or legal claim. It distinguishes administration, counting, audit, certification, challenge, and finality; none is evidence of another unless the applicable authority and record say so.

## Required trace

Every consequential certification-related claim links to: governing jurisdiction and source; responsible office; event and record provenance; the bounded action taken; applicable audit or review record; challenge route; and the precise finality status. A public trace must state what the record establishes and what it does not establish.

## Integrity conditions

- A certification claim cannot substitute for count, audit, or challenge provenance.
- A finality claim must identify its legal basis, scope, timing, and any remaining review route.
- Missing jurisdiction, responsible official, source record, or required lifecycle state blocks an integrity conclusion.
- This model does not alter constitutional, statutory, state, territorial, tribal, or local election authority.
