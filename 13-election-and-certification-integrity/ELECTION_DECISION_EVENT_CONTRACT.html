Election Decision Event Contract

A valid decision event requires a named accountable reviewer, verified reviewer identity, verified independence, completed evidence requirements, disposition of every counterfinding and conflict, explicit scope, effective time, and an append-only event identifier.

Missing or conflicting fields produce BLOCKED_NO_DECISION. Silence, assignment, packet creation, elapsed time, automation, source prominence, or apparent electoral significance cannot create a voter, candidate, ballot, result, certification, elector, contest disposition, officeholder, authority, or effect.
