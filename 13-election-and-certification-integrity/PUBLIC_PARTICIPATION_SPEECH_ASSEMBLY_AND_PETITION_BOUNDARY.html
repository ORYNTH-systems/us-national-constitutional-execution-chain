Public Participation Speech, Assembly, and Petition Boundary

Speech, press, peaceful assembly, petition, observation, advocacy, campaign activity, complaints, and public submissions are protected participation interfaces, not votes, certifications, official decisions, delegations, or governmental effects. Claims require identified conduct, government action, applicable source, jurisdiction, process, decision, remedy, and effective time.

Default state: PARTICIPATION_EVENT_AND_LEGAL_EFFECT_UNADMITTED.
