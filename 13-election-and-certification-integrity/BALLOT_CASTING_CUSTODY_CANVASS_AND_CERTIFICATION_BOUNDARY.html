Ballot Casting, Custody, Canvass, and Certification Boundary

Ballot issuance, casting, secrecy, challenge, cure, custody, tabulation, reconciliation, canvass, audit, recount, and certification are distinct events with distinct actors and records. Aggregates never substitute for ballot-chain integrity, and unofficial reporting never substitutes for authorized certification.

Every material exception remains visible. No result advances while custody, reconciliation, challenge, certifier authority, or finality is unresolved.

Default state: NO_CURRENT_BALLOT_OR_RESULT_ADMITTED.
