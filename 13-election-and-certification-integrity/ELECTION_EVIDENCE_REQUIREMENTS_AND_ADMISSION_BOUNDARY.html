Election Evidence Requirements and Admission Boundary

Each proposed election record must preserve: source-family requirement; competent publisher; official endpoint; jurisdiction; event identity; artifact identity; retrieval time; content hash; promulgation or filing time where applicable; effective time; amendment, repeal, stay, appeal, challenge, and supersession status; corroboration route; predicate supported; limitations; reviewer identity; reviewer independence; decision; and decision time.

Missing fields remain blank, not inferred. An acquired artifact is evidence, not authority; a complete packet is reviewable, not admitted; a reviewer decision is not a legal effect unless the competent real-world authority and operative source independently establish that effect.
