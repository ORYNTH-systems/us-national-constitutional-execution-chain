Election Contest, Elector Appointment, and Counting Boundary

Election administration, judicial or administrative contests, chamber judgment of congressional elections, presidential elector appointment, elector meeting and voting, certificate transmission, congressional counting, objections, contingent selection, and term commencement are separate authority chains.

A projected winner, state certification, elector certificate, congressional count, judicial order, or inauguration-related event does not silently create the others. Each consequence requires its own competent actor, source, record, effective time, challenge status, and finality proof.
