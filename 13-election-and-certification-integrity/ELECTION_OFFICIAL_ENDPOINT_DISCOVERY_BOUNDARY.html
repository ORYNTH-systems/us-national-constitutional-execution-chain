Election Official Endpoint Discovery Boundary

  • Reachability proves transport observation only.
  • An official host does not establish authenticity, completeness, currentness, jurisdiction, event identity, effective time, finality, or legal effect for every artifact it serves.
  • Discovery routers identify competent publishers but do not replace their predicate-specific primary records.
  • Endpoint verification does not create an immutable snapshot, parse facts, identify voters or candidates, establish ballot custody, certify a result, resolve a contest, appoint an elector, count an electoral vote, or admit an officeholder.
  • Unavailable official sources remain explicit and cannot be silently replaced by projections, press reports, campaign statements, social posts, mirrors, or model inference.

Decision effect: NONE.
