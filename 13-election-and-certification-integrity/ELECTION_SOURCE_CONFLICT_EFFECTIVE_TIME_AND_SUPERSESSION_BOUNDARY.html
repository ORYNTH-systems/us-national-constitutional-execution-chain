Election Source Conflict, Effective Time, and Supersession Boundary

Conflicting sources are retained together. Newer publication does not automatically supersede older law; higher apparent rank does not resolve jurisdiction; an order does not establish finality without docket and time proof; and certification does not erase a pending contest or supply a missing authority chain.

Unresolved conflict, scope, effective time, stay, appeal, amendment, repeal, or supersession yields BLOCK. The system records the conflict but cannot select the governing source by confidence, popularity, recency alone, model judgment, or operational convenience.
