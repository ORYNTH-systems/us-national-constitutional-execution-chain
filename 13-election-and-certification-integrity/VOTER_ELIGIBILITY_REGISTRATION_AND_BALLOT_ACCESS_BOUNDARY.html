Voter Eligibility, Registration, and Ballot Access Boundary

Eligibility rules, registration records, voter identity, district assignment, candidacy, qualification, filing, ballot access, and rights claims remain separate evidence objects. A directory entry, form, database match, public statement, party label, or observed ballot does not prove eligibility, registration, candidacy, qualification, access, or a vote.

Race, color, previous condition of servitude, sex, poll-tax, and age-based constitutional boundaries are preserved as distinct claim routes. This batch decides none.
