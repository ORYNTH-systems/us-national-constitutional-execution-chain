# Public Money Chain Model

Public money is not authorized by an objective alone. A consequential public-money chain preserves each distinct transition:

`constitutional and statutory basis -> appropriation -> apportionment/allotment where applicable -> obligation -> contract or other commitment -> payment -> delivered outcome -> receipt -> audit/review`

An appropriation is not itself a contract, obligation, payment, or proof that a resulting effect was authorized. A payment is not proof that delivery was compliant, useful, lawful, or final. Each link has its own accountable actor, amount, purpose, time boundary, and source record.

Unknown appropriation, purpose mismatch, unavailable funds, unbounded obligation, unlinked payment, or missing outcome receipt blocks public-money conformance.
