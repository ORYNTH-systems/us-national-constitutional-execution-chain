# Election and Emergency Conformance

The conformance suite tests structural integrity only. It does not adjudicate election outcomes, factual emergency conditions, or legal disputes.

An election certification path is eligible for the next gate only when its jurisdiction, authority, responsible office, lifecycle provenance, scope, and challenge route are present. An emergency-derived effect is eligible only when the activation has a source-linked predicate, current time and scope, accountable office, review route, and any required restoration receipt.

Every omitted required link is surfaced as `BLOCKED`, `INCOMPLETE`, or `INVALID`; no test vector treats institutional assertion as proof.
