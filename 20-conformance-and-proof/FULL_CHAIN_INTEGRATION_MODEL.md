# Full Constitutional Execution Chain Integration Model

The national chain is evaluated as one linked object, not as isolated records. A consequential public effect is eligible only when the required links can be reconstructed for its path:

Some actions do not traverse every domain. The integration record must identify each applicable domain and expressly record an inapplicability rationale for every omitted standard link. Absence is never presumed to mean satisfied.

## Integrity outcomes

- `BLOCKED`: a prerequisite authority, scope, predicate, evidence, gate, or current status is missing, invalid, stale, or exceeded.
- `INCOMPLETE`: a required provenance, receipt, public trace, or remedy link has not been supplied.
- `ROUTED_FOR_REVIEW`: a conflict, challenge, or condition requires authorized resolution before effect.
- `ELIGIBLE_FOR_EXECUTION`: the record is structurally complete; this is not a legal conclusion or an instruction to act.
- `EXECUTED_WITH_RECEIPT`: an eligible effect has a receipt and continuing review/remedy trace.

This repository does not create public authority, determine constitutional meaning, or make governmental decisions. It makes assertions about execution capable of being tested against the sources and institutions that actually govern them.
