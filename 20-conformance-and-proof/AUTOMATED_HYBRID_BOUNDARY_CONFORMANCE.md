# Automated and Hybrid Boundary Conformance

The conformance suite verifies that systems cannot use capability, access, role labels, or output confidence as substitutes for an accountable principal and a valid execution chain.

For a high-impact effect to be eligible for execution, every referenced record must be present and current: principal, authority, jurisdiction, participation scope, predicate, evidence, consequence gate, review route, remedy route, and receipt route. A system result that fails any prerequisite remains non-effective.

This suite validates structure only. It does not assess model quality, policy desirability, or the legal validity of a real-world action.
