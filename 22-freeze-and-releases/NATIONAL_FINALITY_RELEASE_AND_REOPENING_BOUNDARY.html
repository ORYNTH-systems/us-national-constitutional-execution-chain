National Finality, Release, and Reopening Boundary

Finality requires proof that the competent process reached a terminal posture for the identified object and time. Release separately requires authorized effect scope, a destination, precondition satisfaction, no controlling hold, and a receipt. Reopening requires competent later authority or a proven integrity trigger and preserves the complete prior chain.

Finality is not loss of provenance. Release is not universal effect. Reopening is not silent deletion.
