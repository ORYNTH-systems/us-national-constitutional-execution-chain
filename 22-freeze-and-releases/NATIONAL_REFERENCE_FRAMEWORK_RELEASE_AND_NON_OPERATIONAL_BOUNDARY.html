National Reference Framework Release and Non-Operational Boundary

  • RELEASED means the reference framework version is fixed and reconstructible.
  • RELEASED does not mean evidence candidates are admitted facts.
  • COMPLETE means the planned structural framework layers and fail-closed gates exist.
  • COMPLETE does not mean the 10,759 human-review obligations are resolved.
  • No software agent, model, script, repository, tag, digest, or release artifact possesses governmental authority.
  • No cross-slice interface may produce consequence until its evidence, reviewer, decision, receiving-gate, authorization, remedy, and receipt requirements are independently satisfied.

Operational authority: NONE. Admitted consequential effects: 0.
