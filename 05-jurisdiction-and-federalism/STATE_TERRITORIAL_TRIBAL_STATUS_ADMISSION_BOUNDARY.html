State, Territorial, and Tribal Status Admission Boundary

Names, directories, maps, labels, historical references, statutes, treaties, court opinions, or governmental websites do not alone establish current polity identity, legal status, territory, jurisdiction, recognition, office, membership, sovereignty, immunity, authority, or effect.

Tribal relations require especially explicit separation among polity identity, federal recognition or other status, treaty relations, statutory relations, territorial jurisdiction, membership, inherent authority claims, delegated authority, limits, and current effective law. This batch admits none.
