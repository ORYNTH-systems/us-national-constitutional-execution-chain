Supremacy, Preemption, and Conflict Boundary

Supremacy is not a keyword-triggered erasure of state, territorial, tribal, or local authority. The asserted federal source must be authentic, constitutionally and statutorily grounded, operative, applicable, within scope, and connected to a concrete conflict. Express, field, conflict, obstacle, savings, waiver, immunity, and remedy questions remain separately evidenced. Only an authorized process may resolve a claim and establish effect.
