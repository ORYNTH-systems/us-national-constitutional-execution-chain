Federalism Official Source Policy

Official-source status is publisher-, instrument-, jurisdiction-, and time-specific. A federal source cannot silently establish a state or tribal fact; a state source cannot establish federal operability; a directory cannot establish legal status; and a court opinion cannot be represented beyond its authentic scope, finality, and precedential status.
