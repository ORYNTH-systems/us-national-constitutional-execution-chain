State, Territorial, and Tribal Source Evidence Requirements

Each assertion must identify the specific polity, government, instrument, office, court, territory, relationship, jurisdiction, time interval, and claimed effect. Recognition, sovereignty, immunity, membership, territorial reach, inherent authority, delegated authority, treaty status, compact consent, and operative law remain distinct.
