Bounded pilot readiness

A pilot is ready to begin only when it is intentionally bounded and independently accountable.

  1. Named institutional sponsor with authority to sponsor—not to bypass governing law.
  2. Defined workflow and explicit exclusions.
  3. Approved source policy and evidence-retention boundary.
  4. Human reviewer identity, qualification, independence, and conflict-disclosure model.
  5. Effective-time, supersession, remedy, and escalation rules.
  6. Privacy, security, accessibility, public-records, and retention review appropriate to the institution.
  7. Sandbox or non-consequential environment until separately authorized for live use.
  8. Test cases that include missing evidence, stale evidence, conflict, invalid authority, and attempted unauthorized effect.
  9. Audit receipt and exit criteria.

Failure to establish any readiness condition is a reason to narrow, hold, or decline the pilot—not a reason to waive the condition.
