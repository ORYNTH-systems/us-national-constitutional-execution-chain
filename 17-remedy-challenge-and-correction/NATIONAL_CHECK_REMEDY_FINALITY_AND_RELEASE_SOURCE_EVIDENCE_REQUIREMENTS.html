National Check, Remedy, Finality, and Release Source Evidence Requirements

Each proposed fact must identify the exact object, source, actor, capacity, jurisdiction, process, event time, effective time, affected scope, current review posture, conflicts, counterfindings, terminal condition, and claimed downstream effect. Presentment, consent, judgment, service, compliance, hold, finality, release, correction, and reopening remain distinct evidence objects.
