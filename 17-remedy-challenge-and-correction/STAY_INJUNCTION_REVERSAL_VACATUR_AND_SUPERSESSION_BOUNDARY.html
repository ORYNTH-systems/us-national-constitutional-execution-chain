Stay, Injunction, Reversal, Vacatur, and Supersession Boundary

Temporary restraint, injunction, reversal, vacatur, remand, correction, replacement, revocation, and supersession are distinct event types. Each requires a competent actor, identified target, lawful scope, authenticated record, effective time, duration or terminal condition, service or publication evidence where required, and preserved relationship to the earlier state.

No later event silently rewrites history. Conflicting or incomplete effect claims remain blocked.
