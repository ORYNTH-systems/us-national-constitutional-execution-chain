National Dispute, Remedy, and Correction Lifecycle

The lifecycle is non-automatic. Filing does not prove jurisdiction; jurisdiction does not prove the claim; a decision does not prove finality; finality does not define every downstream effect; and release does not destroy the path by which the result was reached.
